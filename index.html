A small step. A little help.

Support for TaskSnap Focus, your photo task companion for iPhone and iPad.

Contact us

For help, feedback or a privacy request, email samvaiotmp@icloud.com.

Email TaskSnap support

Tell us your device model, iOS version, TaskSnap version and build, what happened and what you expected. A screenshot can help—please remove private information first. Never send passwords or payment details.

Start with one task

On Home, choose Add a task to take a photo, or choose Type it instead. Give it a short title, then add a first small step if that helps. Move the task along your board as you work.

Keep your tasks safe

Settings includes backup, restore and export tools. Make a backup before changing devices or troubleshooting missing tasks. Backups can include photos, so save copies somewhere you trust. If something looks wrong, contact support before deleting the app or resetting data.

iCloud and sharing

Optional iCloud sync and household sharing require a signed-in iCloud account and an internet connection. Check the sync status in Settings. A shared focus room can show your display name, selected task and session goal to other users; keep these free of sensitive information. See the privacy policy for details.

Purchases

For a missing Pro purchase, use the Apple account that made the purchase and choose Restore purchases in the app. Manage subscriptions through Apple subscription settings. Apple handles billing and refunds. Support does not need your payment information.

Testing with TestFlight

Use TestFlight’s feedback tools to report an issue, or email us with the build number and steps to reproduce it. TestFlight builds expire, so check TestFlight for updates. Apple handles TestFlight installation and may share beta diagnostics and feedback with the developer.

Some features require TaskSnap Pro or a supported device. Availability in a beta build can change. TaskSnap is an organization tool and does not provide medical treatment or advice.
